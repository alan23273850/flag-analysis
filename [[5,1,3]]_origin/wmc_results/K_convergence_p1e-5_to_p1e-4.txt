Interpretation
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - K=3 → K=4 brings <0.01 % shift per path at p=1e-4 (~10^(-9)
    absolute), strongly suggesting the true ratio_fail differs from
    K=3 by ~10^(-8) abs / <0.01 % rel at p=1e-4, well below MC's
    statistical noise (~2.5 % from 1600 failures).
  - K=2 truncation error appears to scale ~ p:
       +0.067 % @ 1e-5, +0.33 % @ 5e-5, +0.67 % @ 1e-4
    consistent with the "leading term beyond K=2" being O(p^3).
  - K=3 is the practical sweet spot: ~2.5 min wall for the full
    4-path sweep, far cheaper than MC and accurate to <0.01 %.
